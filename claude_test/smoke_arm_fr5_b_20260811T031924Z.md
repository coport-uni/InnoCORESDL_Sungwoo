Both exceed `MAX_START_APPROACH_DEG` (30 deg), so `arm/replay` refuses
either with a 409 until the arm is jogged near the start pose. And
before replaying a *valve-turning* episode on cell7, confirm the
episode was recorded on that arm — a trajectory recorded at another
workstation sweeps through whatever is actually in front of this one.

There is also a hardware reason the two arms are not interchangeable for
replay, found while diagnosing cell6 (`LearnedPatterns.md` #43): their
**joint-6 soft limits differ** — cell6 `[-175, +175]`, cell7
`[-360, +360]`, everything else identical. An episode recorded on cell7
can therefore contain a wrist angle cell6 will refuse *partway through
the trajectory, with the arm already moving*. The cell does not yet
pre-scan an episode's joint column against the controller's limits.
